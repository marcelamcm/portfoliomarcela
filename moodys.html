moody’s: risk modeler

Product Designer, Nagarro Digital Ventures · Client engagement since Jan 2025

Overview

Moody’s is a global leader in risk intelligence. I help refine and elevate their internal product experiences, focused on untangling complex data journeys and shaping clearer user flows for risk analysts working across 40+ countries.

The work sits inside Nagarro Digital Ventures’ broader engagement with Moody’s, aligning closely with cross-functional teams across the client organization to make dense, high-stakes data tools feel more intuitive without losing their depth.

Building a faster path from idea to prototype

Alongside the core product work, I contribute to RadCode, a pnpm monorepo of browser prototypes (Vite, React, TypeScript) built for rapid UI prototyping. The goal: move from idea to a functional, testable prototype in days rather than weeks.

  • Contributed to a shared package, @radcode/proto-core, and a design system built on Radius components and CSS tokens.
  • Design work included notable prototypes such as Risk Data Refinery (plan-805) and RiskInvestorIQ / RIQ (plan-5566).
  • Followed a rapid discovery-to-testable-prototype cycle, keeping design and engineering tightly looped.

A side investigation: AI-assisted workflow costs

Working this closely with an engineering team building AI-assisted tooling raised a question worth digging into on its own: how do architectural choices, specifically component reusability, affect token consumption in Claude Code (agentic coding assistant) workflows, as distinct from runtime LLM API costs?

That turned into a small analytical framework for evaluating engineering tradeoffs in AI-assisted workflows: tracing where cost is actually paid, identifying the countervailing forces, finding the scaling variable, and grounding the reasoning in the concrete system rather than in general assumptions.
