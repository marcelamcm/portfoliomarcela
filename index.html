I’m Marcela Macedo, a product designer with 10+ years of experience across digital products, design systems, and brand-led experiences. I partner with cross-functional teams to turn complex journeys into clear, intuitive tools for people around the world.

Trained in Brazil, based in Porto, and still happiest inside a system with too many moving parts.

why work with me

My curiosity for design has led me to work across different disciplines, sharpening both my craft and my ability to see the bigger picture. I balance analytical, strategic thinking with a refined visual sensibility, and bring an adaptable, collaborative mindset to every team; listening closely, supporting my peers, and building strong working relationships along the way.
